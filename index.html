SAIL Mathematical Model & Methodology

1. Forecast Demand Projections

Initial carried demand is calculated from current fleet capacity and utilization:
Initial Demand = Current Capacity × (Existing Utilisation / 100)
For horizon Y years with ambition rate g:
Base Demand = Initial Demand × (1 + g)^Y

2. Required Fleet Capacity & Buffer

To safeguard against operational friction and port congestion, capacity is buffered at an 88% target operating load:
Required Capacity = [Forecast Demand × (1 + Buffer%)] / 0.88

3. Three-Layer Commitment Reconciliation

Incremental capacity required is reconciled across demand cases:
• Locked Capacity: max(0, Required_Low - Current_Capacity)
• Flexible Capacity: max(0, Required_Base - max(Current_Capacity, Required_Low))
• Watch Zone Capacity: max(0, Required_High - max(Current_Capacity, Required_Base))
Total High Requirement = Current Fleet + Locked + Flexible + Watch Zone (100% reconciled).

4. Rule-Based Posture Engine

Evaluates market demand growth, business ambition, and competitor additions to output Aggressive Growth, Defensive Growth, Flexible Expansion, or Capacity Preservation.
